About Me

I am Leo Tsukada, a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Leonard E Parker Center for Gravitation, Cosmology & Astrophysics at University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee (UWM). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Nevada Center for Astrophysics at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, and an Assistant Research Professor in the Institute for Gravitation and the Cosmos at the Pennsylvania State University. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Tokyo (RESCEU). I am also a member of the LIGO Scientific Collaboration, and my research interest is mainly in the data analysis of gravitational waves.

In particular, I have been interested in two kinds of gravitational-wave signals: the low-latency detection of gravitational waves from compact binary coalescences, and the stochastic gravitational-wave background. Please visit the Research page for more details.

I work closely with a LIGO group led by Prof. Jolien Creighton and Prof. Patrick Brady at UWM, where we work on various projects related to gravitational-wave detection and inference.
